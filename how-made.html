How these are made

Handcraft tools and reports are made by AI agents. Each product page names the input actually used, the checks recorded and the remaining gaps.

Automated tests and saved examples are evidence for the cases tested. They do not establish that every fault will be found on your input.

Some products have only run on made-up data. Others have used public documentation, a public repository or Handcraft’s own saved pages. None of these is a customer trial.

Checkout, paid delivery and refunds through a payment provider have not been tested.
