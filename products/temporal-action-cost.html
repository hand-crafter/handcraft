Temporal action-cost check

A local tool that reads your prepared Temporal Cloud billing and metrics files and writes one plain report: where actions go, what two design ideas would change, and Cloud versus running it yourself.

On sale soon (download)

Checkout opens soon. Questions: handoverdesk@proton.me.

Who it is for

Backend and platform teams on Temporal Cloud pay-as-you-go that want to see which workflows drive action use.

Price

  • US$199: one-time test price for the download

What you receive

  • A Python runner (3.10 or later, no extra libraries, no network access) with made-up sample inputs and the report they produce
  • A dated 30-day baseline separating actions, storage, support and other charges, with workflow types ranked by estimated actions and the unexplained difference left visible
  • Up to two design ideas you describe, each priced again on the whole account, and a Cloud versus run-it-yourself calculation from your own infrastructure and engineer-time numbers

What it does not do

  • It does not read invoice PDFs or arbitrary CSV layouts: you prepare the documented format from your own exports
  • It covers only public pay-as-you-go Developer or Business pricing, on-demand capacity and a complete 30-day calendar month; anything else stops the report
  • Workflow action figures are estimates, not proof of what the invoice charged for; no forecast and no promised savings
  • It does not judge whether a design change is safe for your application, and no person reviews your report

What it has been run on

Not yet run on a real account export. Its arithmetic was compared with 27 worked examples published in Temporal's pricing and documentation pages and matched all 27; one published credit example is internally inconsistent and is flagged. The saved sample report comes from made-up inputs. The release passed 29 automated tests from a clean copy.

How you receive it

Version 1.0.0 is packaged as handcraft-temporal-action-check-1.0.0.zip and runs on your machine. Nothing is run by Handcraft per order. Checkout opens soon.

What leaves your machine

The tool runs locally on billing and metrics files you prepare, using aliases for workflow names. No account access, workflow histories or payloads are needed, and nothing leaves your machine.
